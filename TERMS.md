## What AmbientMind is

A desktop app that reads the mail, calendars and reminders you connect, and keeps a list of things you need to do. It runs on your computer. There is no account, no subscription and nothing to pay. How it handles your data is described in the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).

- **Changes are yours.** Changes it makes to your Reminders or to its own Google calendar happen because you asked, or because you turned on a feature that does them. Every change in the app can be undone, but changes already synced to Apple or Google are subject to those services.
- **Use it lawfully.** Only connect accounts you are allowed to use, and use AmbientMind in line with the terms of those services (Google, Apple, Ollama and the model providers).
